The honest cost

This is the least distinctive of the three in a toolbar. Warm paper reads beautifully at 112px and reads as a generic document icon at 16. The serif also sits oddly against Firefox chrome, which is a sans everywhere.

It carries the same two costs Blueprint does, and the board should say so rather than let them pass. The dark cut is a separate asset, so this ships two icon files. And the sheets are filled in order to overlap, so that fill is standing in for the toolbar color and will show as a pale rectangle on a custom theme.

Pick this if the listing page and the extension's voice matter more to you than the toolbar slot.
