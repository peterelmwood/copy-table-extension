Two SVG files, both self-theming. Everything below is what is actually in src/icons/, not a mockup of it.

The two cuts

table.svg 48 grid · serves 32 and up
table-16.svg 16 grid · magnified 6× here

The 16 cut drops both body rules and the second column, and thickens what is left. It is a different drawing, not a smaller one.

Why the copy is masked, not covered

filled
masked

The board flagged this as a cost of the direction. It is fixed rather than shipped: a mask knocks the plate-shaped hole out of the dashed copy, so nothing paints a background and the mark sits on any theme.

Theme response

light theme · ink #22283A · copy #3D7FB8
dark theme · ink #E3E6EE · copy #6BA8D8

One prefers-color-scheme block inside each SVG, so there is no light/dark asset pair and no theme_icons mapping to get backwards.
