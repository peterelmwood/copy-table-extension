The honest cost

Hairlines are the whole character, and hairlines are exactly what 16px destroys. The 16 cut above already had to drop a rule and a tier of detail, and you ship two icon files rather than one.

There is a subtler cost. The front table is filled so it can hide the dashed copy behind it, which means the fill is really the toolbar color in disguise. On a custom Firefox theme that becomes a visible rectangle. The production asset would need to clip the copy rather than paint over it.

Pick this if the audience is people who read the permissions list before installing.
